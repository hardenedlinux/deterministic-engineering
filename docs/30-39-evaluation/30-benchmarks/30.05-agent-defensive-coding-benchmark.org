:PROPERTIES:

:STATUS: implemented-verified

all four AI Agent defensive-hallucination classes (=G2= in [[file:../../10-19-design/10-architecture/10.153-defensive-engineering-taxonomy-and-modular-organization.org][10.153]]) without
generating false positives on clean deterministic-boundary code.

  expected outcome (=pass= vs. =fail= + exact hallucination/anti-evasion/escalation/durable-resume class). Candidate
  auditor output never generates gold.

* Limits
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: bench3005-limits
:END:

This 65-case benchmark establishes deterministic AST-level, diff-delta,
scope-fencing, bounded closed-loop, hot-policy co-evolution, async-cut,
semantic-drift memoization, AEAD-isolated Merkle storage, role-projected
context, multi-agent SSI concurrency, and business SoD/replay precision and
recall on its declared TypeScript, Scheme, C, C++, and Rust cohort. It does not
claim interprocedural dataflow completeness, whole-program alias analysis, or
coverage of arbitrary obfuscated evasion patterns across external repositories.
