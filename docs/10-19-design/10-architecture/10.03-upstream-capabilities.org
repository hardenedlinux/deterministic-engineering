** Checked relational language and candidate boundary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ascent-language
:END:

Sources: [[file:../../../.gerbil/pkg/github.com/tao3k/gerbil-ascent/program/interface.ss][public Scheme interface]], [[file:../../../.gerbil/pkg/github.com/tao3k/gerbil-ascent/docs/library-acceptance.org][Library admission contract]],
[[file:../../../.gerbil/pkg/github.com/tao3k/gerbil-ascent/candidate/reasoning.ss][candidate execution and receipt binding]].

The public interface exports the checked relational language and operator/session
facilities. The contract describes finite program values, positive atoms,
lower-stratum negation, a fixed filtering/computation subset, checked reductions,
finite pointwise views, and min/max lattice declarations. Trusted Scheme constructs
program values before admission; arbitrary host callbacks are not accepted as
checked rule bodies.

The inert candidate path is narrower. It accepts data in a declared subset,
rejecting host value expressions, arbitrary callbacks, finite views, and lattice
declarations. This distinction matters when an Agent proposes new rules: a trusted
host program and an untrusted candidate are different admission surfaces.

~reasoning-receipt-bound?~ checks receipt type, snapshot validity, identity,
generation, snapshot digest, and candidate digest. Its source comments explicitly
limit digest binding to local content consistency, not source authority.
~reasoning-attempt~ distinguishes complete, rejected, and unknown outcomes. Optional
finite or founded evidence has a separate verifier and completion boundary.

For the engineering model this supports explicit facts, scoped rule admission,
bound candidate execution, and method-specific result checking. The engineering
vocabulary, authority rules, obligation templates, and admission semantics are
still project-owned work. Native admission does not generally prove termination;
finite rule structure alone is insufficient for unrestricted scalar recursion.

** Update and invalidation granularity
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ascent-updates
:END:

Source: [[file:../../../.gerbil/pkg/github.com/tao3k/gerbil-ascent/docs/scheme-session-deletion-design.org][native dependency invalidation and atomic deletion contract]].

The contract assigns replacement validation to ~program/admission.ss~, eligibility
and dependency closure to ~program/update-selection.ss~, and completed-result reuse
to ~program/reuse.ss~. Eligible source replacement recomputes affected downstream
relations, including recursive components, while independent completed relations
seed the new engine. Negation, reductions, and lattice projections participate in
impact analysis. Failed prospective updates retain the previous completed state.

This is relation-level invalidation and affected-component rederivation. It is
not universal fact-level deletion maintenance. Closed builder-created procedures
qualify for the reuse interpretation; opaque generators, callbacks, patterns, and
custom storage providers require full prospective recomputation. General deletion
speedup and complete lineage are not implied.

Engineering change propagation can adopt this conservative shape, but must add
dependencies for requirements, contracts, profiles, evidence producers, acceptance
policies, and decisions. A relational evaluator cannot infer these domain edges
from arbitrary prose or arbitrary external artifacts.

** Local evidence and interaction
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ascent-evidence
:END:

Sources: [[file:../../../.gerbil/pkg/github.com/tao3k/gerbil-ascent/docs/features/interactive-evidence-reasoning.org][interactive evidence contract]],
[[file:../../../.gerbil/pkg/github.com/tao3k/gerbil-ascent/docs/features/candidate-feedback/acceptance.org][candidate feedback qualification contract]].

The interaction contract describes registered finite local predicates, explicit
bindings, current source/result references, and supported/contradicted/unknown
feedback. Models select operations and bindings; they do not change registered
rules or submit arbitrary Scheme through that interaction surface. Source
replacement invalidates references. Evidence dependencies and the temporal
interaction trajectory serve distinct purposes.

Candidate feedback compares completed, bound results with a caller-owned finite
reference or same-source gold result. A match applies to that query and source;
it does not establish the reference's authority or natural-language fidelity.
A wrong gold compared with itself can match. The contract documents a bounded
finite Scheme/Lean/Quint correspondence corpus; it explicitly excludes general
compiler correctness, concurrent source mutation, and model-language intent.

For this repository, a reusable checked fragment may support a design subclaim.
The architecture argument and obligation policy must still determine what that
subclaim means and whether its support is sufficient for the engineering decision.
Model benefit or whole-task success is not inferred from local support.

** Typed construction and precedence
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: lean-composition
:END:

Sources: [[leanpoo:README.org][project scope]], [[leanpoo:LeanPoo/Object/Definition.lean][typed object definitions]],
[[leanpoo:docs/contracts/c3-graph.org][ordinary C3 graph coherence contract]].

~Object.defineIn~ builds typed declarations in an existing schema with ordered
parents through the C4 construction path. Strict variants reject duplicate direct
writes before compilation. First-class parent composition is available through
~Memoized.defineFrom~; the result retains the plan and receiver resolution mode.
These are typed Lean constructions, not a Scheme parser or a domain DSL.

The ordinary C3 contract lists complete cached/reference result equality on finite
graphs, including errors, and semantic certificates for graph interpretation.
This ordinary C3 result must not be silently identified with all C4 extensions or
all engineering contract semantics.

The upstream README explicitly permits divergence of general open-recursive
computation. Bounded debug reads do not establish termination of arbitrary object
observations. Our design snapshot therefore needs its own completion and effect
policy before exposing effective facts to ASCENT.

** Explicit structural contract strengthening
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: lean-contracts
:END:

Source: [[leanpoo:LeanPoo/Functional/ObjectContractRefinement.lean][ObjectContract refinement]].

~refine~ retains the original shape, constraints, and obligation phase, then adds
a gated phase. ~refine_accepts_iff~ states that acceptance requires the original
contract and the added phase on the same value/path. ~refine_preserves_rejection~
prevents refinement from admitting a value rejected by the original contract.
~diagnose_refine~ and ~refine_assoc~ preserve the specified sequencing semantics.

The source explicitly states that ordinary descriptor inheritance remains
override. These laws concern a supplied pure interpretation. They do not prove
behavioral substitutability of a changed gateway, nor authorize a child to weaken
an inherited security guarantee without new compatibility work.

** Dependency-certified proof transport
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: lean-proof-reuse
:END:

Sources: [[leanpoo:LeanPoo/Proof/Types.lean][proof types]], [[leanpoo:LeanPoo/Proof/Patch.lean][patches]],
[[leanpoo:LeanPoo/Proof/Reuse.lean][reuse and closure]], [[leanpoo:LeanPoo/Proof/Invalidation.lean][pending obligations]],
[[leanpoo:Examples/ProofReuse.lean][independent quota-change example]].

~Obligation~ stores typed dependencies, a proposition over state, and a ~stable~
proof transporting that proposition across equality of its dependencies.
~Patch~ stores a state transformer, touched keys, a ~frame~ proof for untouched
keys, and added obligations. Sequential patches compose their transformation and
write footprint. ~Certificate~ covers every obligation owned by the proof object.

~unaffected~ requires dependencies to avoid touched keys. ~reuse~ transports an old
owned proof under that premise. ~close~ needs fresh proofs for affected old and
added obligations. ~pending~, ~mem_pending_iff~, and ~closePending~ connect the
executable pending list with that criterion. The example changes quota while
retaining an identity theorem whose dependency is unchanged.

This is stronger than trusting a declared list alone: ~stable~ and ~frame~ are
proof obligations. However, interpreting all relevant engineering inputs as typed
state, extracting dependencies from Scheme or external tools, and establishing
artifact validity are not supplied automatically.

** Registered graph invalidation
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: lean-invalidation
:END:

Sources: [[leanpoo:LeanPoo/Proof/Invalidation.lean][reverse graph closure]],
[[leanpoo:LeanPoo/Object/CacheInvalidation.lean][revision cache invalidation]].

~invalidatedNodes~ propagates through registered reverse inheritance edges.
~invalidateCaches_descendant~ clears reached descendants under its path/bound
premises; ~invalidateCaches_once~ advances an affected owner once;
~invalidateCaches_frame~ preserves unaffected owners. ~guardedEdit~ and
~publishEdit_sealed~ describe refusal and preservation of the old source.

The file explicitly requires correct edge registration, sequential evaluation,
and graph finiteness. It does not certify arbitrary concurrent mutation or a
complete engineering evidence graph. These premises become integration obligations
rather than hidden assumptions in our model.

ASCENT is an evidence-chain-driven reasoning system. It owns current support,
source/premise applicability, reusable derivation components, invalidation and
observable Trajectory feedback. The domain rules in this repository are a
downstream specialization, not the entirety of ASCENT or a second evidence engine.

The inspected Scheme rules join evidence and factors by observation ID/digest,
retain support and counterevidence, keep hypotheses conditional, derive conflict
and unknown, compute view deltas, and acknowledge displayed units. Registration
does not authenticate observations or grant authority. A query's local unknown
does not prove global absence. Selected facts and associations remain downstream
DSL responsibilities; Python is transport rather than a relevance engine.

The trajectory contract distinguishes available reads and the implemented thin
event index from the richer arbitrary relation-delta/handoff contract still needing
qualification. The application must expose and test the actual interface; no
mandatory navigator, hypothesis ranking or closed action menu follows from evidence.

The committed [[smp:docs/smp-trajectory-application.md][SMP application contract]] supplies an existing downstream example
of this discipline. Native SMP acceptance is not reassessed here. The
[[file:10.04-evidence-driven-workflow.org][primary architecture document]] specifies the gateway mapping and makes QNT the
maintained authoring language with generated TLC backend artifacts, alongside Lean
and source/native correspondence. Specula/TLAPS remain reference inputs.

The inspected Core revision owns native Module values, schemas, SourceRef,
selection and shared composition mechanisms. Its role-indexed authoring Contract
covers types, objects, funs, config and interface responsibilities. Product identity
and policy are caller-owned. Native POO supplies ancestry; Core does not introduce
a second general merge evaluator. Explicit graph contributions run once in order;
their receipts do not claim idempotence or convergence.

The bounded ~use-module~ form binds an already available native scope once and
preserves its identity without forcing source, prototype or Profile slots merely
to bind an alias. Profile selection is distinct from a bounded expression body.
Retaining a source slot does not authenticate its origin or authorize execution.

~module-test-projection~ takes the existing selected Graph and validates requested
owners. It observes test directories and Scheme helpers, selects ~*-test.ss~
inputs, and exposes currentness checks. Normal and integration suites are distinct.
The native controls include empty-selection refusal, unselected poison sources,
test additions/edits, and source-window separation around package effects.
These controls were inspected, not run here.

~poo-flow-test-case~ projects through native ~std/test~ with a POO policy for
duration and memory watchdogs. Process-wide heap sampling is not independent
per-thread allocation attribution. Import/discovery, selection, explicit execution,
natural process completion, and accepted engineering evidence remain separate
events. A clean selected suite cannot qualify an unselected domain.

The [[file:../../20-29-cases/20-gateway/20.03-gateway-design-audit.org::#audit-source-boundary][subsequent audit]] distinguishes this committed Graph-based API from
uncommitted root-selection/lazy-Graph and qualification-suite edits observed in
the live worktree. It also records package dependency mismatches that must be
resolved before an integration demonstration can qualify the chosen combination.

These mechanisms support organizing engineering concerns as Modules, reusable
policy as Profiles, and bounded executable work as Scenarios/Cases. Organization
roles, standards clauses and assurance policy remain domain objects. Their meaning
must not be inferred merely from directory placement or a Module name.
