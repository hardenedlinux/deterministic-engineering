* Purpose: Composing Foundational Governance Into High-Stakes Business Operations
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: biz158-purpose
:STATUS: implemented-verified
:END:

Documents [[file:10.153-defensive-engineering-taxonomy-and-modular-organization.org][10.153]]–[[file:10.157-durable-ai-context-projection-and-multi-agent-ssi-concurrency.org][10.157]] established the formal, language, persistence, and
concurrency primitives of Durable AI Governance. This document demonstrates how
those exact primitives compose without new ad-hoc frameworks into two
high-stakes enterprise business domains:

1. *Scenario B1 — Autonomous Multi-Agent Production Change, SoD Release & Durable Rollback*:
   Composes =governance-sod= (Clark & Wilson 1987 Separation of Duties),
   pinned =M1= pre-release Merkle cuts, live =M3= emergency-freeze checks,
   epistemic canary SLO states (=Healthy= vs. =PendingScope= vs. =Breached=),
   and monotonic =M3= lease fencing on rollback—while keeping 100% of failed
   rollout forensics pinned across GC.
2. *Scenario B2 — Multi-Tenant Regulated Decision Workflow & Deterministic Regulatory Replay*:
   Composes per-tenant =AES-256-GCM= AEAD partitions (=R5=), non-serializable
   =AeadPartitionKey= (=R3=), pinned =M1= decision proof cuts (=F2=), and
   version-pinned =M2= policy/model digests (=R4=) to guarantee zero cross-tenant
   leakage and cryptographically verifiable multi-year decision replay after
   raw chat history compaction and garbage collection.

* Limits
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: biz158-limits
:END:

Both business orchestrators verify the logical SoD, cut-pinning, fencing,
AEAD-isolation, and deterministic-replay invariants in-process. Production
deployment additionally requires external KMS/HSM custody of tenant AEAD keys,
signed human approval identities (e.g. WebAuthn/OIDC), and irreversible
external side-effect compensation hooks (e.g. multi-region DB schema migrations).
