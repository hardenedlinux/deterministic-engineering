* Overview
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: arch10172-overview
:END:

Across Phases 1–23, our 19-model Quint/Apalache defense fleet proved critical
safety invariants and generated countermodels on **bounded** state spaces (e.g.,
=N = 4, f = 1, t = 3= for =B10= and two epochs =E1 -> E2= for =B10b=). While
bounded symbolic model checking and 100% MBT SUT refinement excel at finding
protocol counterexamples and verifying concrete state-machine transitions, they
cannot by themselves establish universal algebraic safety across **unbounded**
parameters (=∀ N, f, t, E, k=).

Phase 24 bridges bounded model checking to **unbounded machine-checked proofs**
in Lean 4 ([[file:../../../packages/lean-models/DeterministicEngineering/Engineering/DurableGovernanceQuorum.lean][DurableGovernanceQuorum.lean]])
with zero =sorry=, zero =sorryAx=, zero custom axioms, and zero Mathlib
dependencies, paired with an automated live Lean 4 proof-carrying oracle
([[file:../../../languages-engineering/ts/defense/agent-audit/lean-proof-oracle.ts][lean-proof-oracle.ts]])
and 5 new benchmark cases (=AG126=–=AG130=, reaching **130 total cases**).

- This repository (=deterministic-engineering-qnt=) is a reusable **Library**:
  it depends strictly on Lean 4 core + =LeanPoo= (zero Mathlib) for fast,

* Four-Paradigm Heterogeneous Verification Stack
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: arch10172-four-paradigms
:END:

With Phase 24 complete, the durable AI governance architecture is independently
verified across four orthogonal formal methods:

1. **Quint / Apalache (19 defense models, 56 canonical models)**: Bounded
   symbolic safety, countermodels, =F3= =guard-probe= necessity, and 100% MBT
   SUT state-machine isomorphism.
2. **Gerbil Scheme ASCENT Datalog (20 sources × 12 outputs)**: Live heterogeneous
   cross-runtime =gxi= fixed-point auditing over cold =M1= =B1=–=B10= evidence.
3. **CBMC (C boundary harness)**: Bounded memory, pointer, and overflow proof.
4. **Lean 4 (=DurableGovernanceQuorum.lean= + =lean-proof-oracle.ts=)**:
   Unbounded inductive/algebraic theorems with automated =#print axioms=
   certification and G2 anti-fabrication rejection of =sorry= / custom axioms.

* Limits
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: arch10172-limits
:END:

These Lean 4 theorems certify the abstract mathematical models of quorum
intersection, multi-epoch handover chains, =R3= reachability, linear budget
conservation, and attenuation chains. Cryptographic assumptions (Ed25519
unforgeability, SHA-256 collision resistance, AES-256-GCM authenticity) and
host OS memory isolation remain outside the algebraic proof kernel.
