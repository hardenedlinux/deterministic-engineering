* Decision
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: qnt151-decision
:STATUS: implemented-verified
:END:

Reusable finite-state threat and systems-engineering models are declared once as
co-located Gerbil Scheme POO AST modules mounted via Yuanxu's multi-window
=init.ss= directly inside =packages/quint-models/<domain>/<subdomain>/<model>/=
and projected into self-contained pure =.qnt= files and =README.org= documents.
Downstream repositories install the projected model mirror and package-aware
resolver through bun. Official =@informalsystems/quint= (=0.33.0=) is used
unmodified.

| Concern | Owner |
|---------+-------|
| Model semantics: typed AST state, guards, transitions, emergent safety, traces, auto-derived countermodels | Co-located Yuanxu units under =packages/quint-models/<domain>/<subdomain>/<model>/{types,objects,interface}.ss= |
| Typed AST validator, automatic guard-pruning countermodel derivation, Quint & README projection, sealed plan contracts | =library/quint-methods/= (=ast.ss=, =types.ss=, =objects.ss=, =methods.ss=), pure |
| 3-axis orthogonal Profile Namespace (=assurance.*=, =bound.*=, =governance.*=) & Yuanxu Design 165 convergence | =library/quint-profiles/= (=types.ss=, =objects.ss=, =interface.ss=, =profile-catalog:=), pure |
| User selection | =programs.deterministic-engineering.languages.quint= in [[file:../../../config.ss][config.ss]] (default ='assurance.bounded-proof=) |
| Projected models & docs | =@deterministic-engineering/quint-models= under =packages/quint-models/= (37 pure =.qnt= models across 10 subdomains, zero JSON sidecars) |
| Package-aware import resolution, transparent =@quint-extends=/=@quint-const=, AST composition, =run-plan=, =run-fleet=, =mbt-audit= & ITF verification | =@deterministic-engineering/quint-modules-resolver= under =packages/quint-modules-resolver/= |
| Dynamic projection & qualification execution | =t/support/project-quint-models.ss=, =t/yanxu/<Mount>/<owner>/qualification-test.ss=, and consuming hosts |

Placements follow [[file:10.150-repository-organization-and-package-taxonomy.org][10.150]]. The Library boundary of [[file:10.148-flat-declarative-library-boundary.org][10.148]] holds: neither
=library/quint-methods/= nor =library/quint-profiles/= nor any co-located model
unit launches a process, writes a file, or issues an execution verdict. All
imports across modules use canonical Gerbil modular paths
(=:deterministic-engineering/...=), never cross-module relative =../= paths.

* Why Typed Scheme POO AST Projection Rather Than Quint Modules Alone
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: qnt151-why
:END:

Quint instantiation substitutes =const= values only. It cannot inherit a module,
structurally prune a single guard to synthesize a weakened countermodel, refine
an action, or enforce a monotonic governance floor across a fleet of models.
Those are POO + typed AST + Yuanxu Design 165 operations:

1. *Typed S-expression AST DSL (=library/quint-methods/ast.ss=)*: every stateful
   model encodes its guards, transitions, emergent safety predicate, and traces
   as typed Scheme AST forms validated against declared state fields before any
   =.qnt= text is emitted.
2. *Automatic guard-pruning countermodel derivation*: every critical guard is
   marked with =(guard <id> <expr> [fallback])=. Declaring
   =derive-from-transition:= and =dropped-guard:= automatically rewrites the
   hardened transition AST to drop that single guard and emits a weakened
   =step*= countermodel action alongside its =// @countermodel= ITF assertion.
3. *Emergent relational invariants*: safety predicates assert direct relational
   properties over protocol state (for example, partition conservation,
   monotonic storage, non-disjoint ownership exclusion, or capability
   isolation) rather than synthetic =unsafe= boolean flags.

* 3-Axis Orthogonal Profile Namespace (=library/quint-profiles/=)
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: qnt151-profiles
:END:

Built on Yuanxu Design 165 (=profile-catalog:= auto-discovery, =by-tag=
targeting, Band-5 monotonic =governance-floor= meet lattice, and
zero-recompilation runtime overlays), =library/quint-profiles/= replaces the
earlier monolithic =threat-model-profiles= with three strictly orthogonal axes:

| Axis | Stage | Profiles | Semantics |
|------+-------+----------+-----------|
| =assurance.*= | =runtime= | =assurance.syntax-only=, =assurance.deterministic-trace=, =assurance.bounded-proof=, =assurance.mbt-oracle= | *HOW* thoroughly to check; switchable cold via =apply-quint-runtime-profile= without recompiling |
| =bound.*= | =assembly= | =bound.fast=, =bound.standard=, =bound.compact=, =bound.extended-counterexample=, =bound.deep-counterexample= | *WHAT* finite state-diameter envelope (=verify-depth= / =countermodel-depth=); immutable at runtime |
| =governance.*= | =assembly= (Band 5 meet) | =governance.mandatory-countermodels=, =governance.zero-trust-assurance= | *NON-NEGOTIABLE* monotonic =(or a b)= floor on =quint.property.stateful=; no peer profile can weaken =require-countermodels?= back to =#f= |

* Projection, Plan & Configuration
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: qnt151-methods
:END:

=library/quint-methods/= exports three pure CLOS generics dispatched on
prototype ancestry (=ThreatModel.=):

- =(.defgeneric quint-module (model))= validates =QuintDomainModelType= + AST
  state references and emits self-contained pure =.qnt= text with embedded
  =// @quint-model=, =// @quint-version=, =// @apalache-version=, =// @verify=,
  =// @countermodel=, and =// Limits:= directives.
- =(.defgeneric quint-model-readme (model))= emits each model's =README.org=
  directly from the POO metadata, invariant AST, countermodel table, and paper
  references.
- =(.defgeneric quint-plan (model profile policy))= validates the sealed
  =QuintProfileType= + =QuintPolicyType= contract and returns a sealed
  =QuintPlanType= object; stateful models with =require-countermodels?: #t= and
  zero countermodels are rejected.

* Packages and Tooling
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: qnt151-packages
:END:

** =@deterministic-engineering/quint-models=
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: qnt151-quint-models
:END:

Distributed via =git subtree split --prefix=packages/quint-models=. Every
model ships a self-contained pure =.qnt= file with embedded header directives
and zero redundant =plan.json= / =receipt.json= sidecars; exact mirror commit
SHAs in downstream =package.json= + =bun.lock= provide immutable content
provenance, while DE's dynamic Yuanxu projection script
([[file:../../../t/support/project-quint-models.ss][project-quint-models.ss]]) and per-model qualification suites verify zero drift
between Scheme POO sources, =.qnt=, and =README.org=.

** =@deterministic-engineering/quint-modules-resolver=
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: qnt151-quint-modules-resolver
:END:

Wraps official =@informalsystems/quint= (=0.33.0=, exact peer dependency,
unmodified) with package-aware bare-specifier resolution plus lean modular
composition and verification workflows (keeping full multi-prototype POO
inheritance and guard-pruning strictly in Gerbil Scheme):

- =typecheck=, =test=, =run= (including =--mbt= ITF trace generation), and
  =verify= (Apalache =0.62.1=) with Node-style =exports= resolution and
  transparent single-parent =// @quint-extends= + =// @quint-const= expansion
  (zero intermediate =.tmp/= files required).
- =run-plan <Model.qnt> [--verify]= & =run-fleet <dir> [--domain=...] [--subdomain=...] [--concurrency=N]=:
  parses embedded =// @verify= and =// @countermodel= directives, runs
  deterministic traces + bounded Apalache safety + inherited/declared
  countermodel refutations, and evaluates pure zero-=eval= ITF predicates
  ([[file:../../../packages/quint-modules-resolver/src/itf.ts][itf.ts]]).
- =mbt-audit <Model.qnt> [--min-coverage=0.5] [--json]=: parses =action step=
  from Quint's AST, runs =--mbt= traces, and fails closed if action coverage
  falls below threshold.
- =compose <base> <overlay> --out <Derived.qnt>= & =expand <Model.qnt> [--out <Expanded.qnt>]=:
  performs structural Quint AST extension (differential-tested against DE's
  =gerbil-parser= SpanEdit composer on live Apalache proofs).
- =list= and =verify-deps=: discovers and verifies all installed
  =@deterministic-engineering/quint-models= modules against the pinned Quint
  version.

* Limits
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: qnt151-limits
:END:

Projected models are finite abstractions; trusted bits summarize real
verifiers, storage, and networks. Bounded model checking and MBT ITF trace
replay establish bounded conformance over modeled state transitions — not
unbounded liveness, cryptographic proofs, physical storage atomicity, or full
compiler/runtime refinement.
