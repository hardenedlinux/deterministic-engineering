* Overview
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: arch10171-overview
:END:

Phases 21–22 ([[file:10.169-proactive-threshold-witness-cosigning-and-quorum-finality.org][10.169]],
[[file:10.170-full-b1-b10-declarative-quorum-closure-and-n-version-proof-carrying-audit.org][10.170]])
established proactive Ed25519 threshold witness cosigning (=2f+1= of =3f+1=) and
declarative ASCENT quorum finality over a **static** witness committee. In
long-lived orthogonally persistent AI governance systems (=gerbil-persist=)
operating over months or years, a static committee is operationally unsafe:
witnesses caught equivocating (e.g., =w4-byz= in =AG116=) must be evicted, keys
must rotate across epochs, and retired committees pose a **Long-Range /
Posterior-Corruption** risk once departing members lose skin in the game.

Business Extension **=B10b=** closes this long-horizon cryptographic frontier
with three coupled mechanisms:

1. **Joint-Consensus Dual-Quorum Handover (=Q_old ∩ Q_new=)**: Following Ongaro
   & Ousterhout (USENIX ATC 2014, Raft §6), transitioning from =Epoch E=
   committee =C_old= to =Epoch E+1= committee =C_new= requires a
   =JointHandoverCertificate= signed by **BOTH** =>= t_old= distinct Ed25519
   witnesses of =C_old= **AND** =>= t_new= distinct Ed25519 witnesses of =C_new=
   binding exact monotonic =fromEpoch -> fromEpoch + 1=, both canonical roster
   digests, and the RFC 6962 history anchor =(anchorTreeSize, anchorRootHashHex)=.
2. **Forward-Secure Retired Key Zeroization (=R3= =M3= Key Evolution)**:
   Following Bellare & Miner (CRYPTO 1999), =ForwardSecureWitnessKey= isolates
   per-epoch Ed25519 private keys in a non-serializable =M3= capability and
   provides irreversible =zeroizeRetiredEpochKey(epoch)= so posterior host
   compromise cannot forge past-epoch signatures.
3. **Offline Weak-Subjectivity Monotonic Epoch Pinning & RFC 6962 Continuity**:
   =ForwardSealedEpochVerifier= monotonically pins the latest verified handover
   epoch and refuses retired-epoch checkpoints, alternative retired handovers
   (even if =>= 2f_old+1= retired keys are later bought/compromised), future
   unhanded-over epochs, and spliced Epoch =E+1= trees failing RFC 6962
   append-only consistency from the pinned handover anchor.

* Limits
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: arch10171-limits
:END:

Weak-subjectivity epoch pinning protects all online and periodically syncing
verifiers against posterior corruption of retired committees. A brand-new
verifier bootstrapping from genesis after many years of complete offline
isolation still requires an out-of-band pinned checkpoint hash (the fundamental
weak-subjectivity bound of proof-of-stake / committee-reconfigured ledgers).
