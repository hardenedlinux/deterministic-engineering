* Problem: Eliminating the "Trusted Coordinator / Honest Storage Operator" Assumption
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: tlog166-problem
:STATUS: implemented-verified
:END:

Business pillars =B1=–=B8= ([[file:10.158-durable-ai-business-extensions-sod-release-and-regulated-replay.org][10.158]]–[[file:10.165-durable-memory-poisoning-defense-and-atms-causal-retraction.org][10.165]]) defend against malicious
agents, compromised contractors, stale human approvals, prompt-injected data
exfiltration, and poisoned long-term memory—yet still assume the *Coordinator*
updating the single =M3= root cell is honest.

A compromised Coordinator can execute a *Split-View Equivocation Attack*
(presenting a clean audit root =R_clean= to an external regulator while feeding
a backdoored root =R_backdoor= to production release workers), retroactively
rewrite historical leaves, or silently roll back the log size. Content-addressed
Merkle DAGs alone cannot detect equivocation without append-only consistency
proofs and independent witness gossip (Laurie, Langley & Kasper RFC 6962;
Syta et al. IEEE S&P 2016).

* Architecture (Scenario B9): RFC 6962 Merkle Log × Asymmetric Ed25519 STHs
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: tlog166-architecture
:STATUS: implemented-verified
:END:

| Governance Mechanism | Cryptographic / Formal Construction | Security Guarantee |
|----------------------+-------------------------------------+--------------------|
| Exact RFC 6962 Merkle Tree | Domain-separated hashing (=0x00 \|\| leaf=, =0x01 \|\| left \|\| right=), =O(log N)= Inclusion Proofs (=PATH=) & Append-Only Consistency Proofs (=SUBPROOF=) | Proves any historical governance receipt is included AND every new tree strictly extends all prior trees with zero retroactive mutation |
| Non-Serializable =M3= Ed25519 =CoordinatorSigningKey= (=R3=) | Real =node:crypto= Ed25519 keypair; private key branded with =M3_NON_SERIALIZABLE_CAPABILITY=; external regulators verify Signed Tree Heads using *only* the public key PEM | Zero shared secret required for external regulatory verification; private key cannot leak into =M1= storage |
| Witness Gossip Equivocation Detector (=WitnessEquivocationMonitor=) | Checks every STH against observed sizes & RFC 6962 consistency; emits self-authenticating =EquivocationFraudProof= containing *both* coordinator-signed STHs on divergence, rewrite, or rollback | Makes coordinator misbehavior cryptographically provable to any third party ("Honest or Bust") |

* Limits
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: tlog166-limits
:STATUS: implemented-verified
:END:

Equivocation detection guarantees accountability whenever at least two honest
witnesses (or a witness and a client/regulator) can gossip observed Signed Tree
Heads. Under a total network eclipse isolating a victim permanently from all
external witnesses, split-view detection is deferred until gossip reconnects
(or prevented proactively by requiring threshold witness cosigning before
production release promotion).
