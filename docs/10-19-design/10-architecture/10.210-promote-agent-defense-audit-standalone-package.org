* Architectural Decision (Zero Legacy)

AI coding anti-defensive-programming governance is a cross-language platform
concern covering TypeScript/JavaScript, C, C++, Rust, Go, and Gerbil Scheme —
not a TypeScript-only language example. Keeping the 6-language =G2= auditor,
closed-loop scope-fenced repair harness (=G5=), and LeanPoo fleet verifier
inside =languages-engineering/ts/defense/agent-audit/= violated repository
responsibility boundaries:

- =languages-engineering/<lang>/= is strictly reserved for per-language contrast
  subjects and demos (=c/=, =cpp/=, =go/=, =rust/=, =ts/defense/boundary-contrast/=).
- Authoritative semantic SSOT remains language-neutral in Gerbil Scheme
  POO/ASCENT (=library/defensive-boundaries/=, =library/defensive-governance/=,
  =library/defense-profiles/=), Quint (=packages/quint-models/engineering/defense/=),
  and Lean 4 (=packages/lean-models/=).
- The portable, zero-dependency multi-language execution engine is promoted to a
  first-class 5th publishable distribution package:
  [[file:../../../packages/agent-defense-audit/package.json][packages/agent-defense-audit/]]
  (=@deterministic-engineering/agent-defense-audit=), and the legacy directory
  =languages-engineering/ts/defense/agent-audit/= is deleted with zero
  compatibility shims.
