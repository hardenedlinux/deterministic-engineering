* DESIGN Objective and three independent verdicts
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: s9-case-joint-acceptance
:STATUS: Existing-file/entry/evidence/through-gate design only; no implementation, acceptance or delivery
:END:

S9 refines WP10's G0-G8 without renumbering the implementation stages. Bind exact
source/build/loaded inputs, independent required controls and actual owner-issued
artifacts across S1-S8, then derive a joint report. The future orchestrator invokes
real framework operations; it cannot mint private results, acceptance or authority.

Keep three verdicts separate: (1) mechanism/control correctness at specified source,
compiled, cold, recovery and receiver cuts; (2) actual DE-CASE-01 semantic/technical/
role/authority conditions at its declared scope; (3) research comparison between
candidate designs. A correctly refused forged request can pass its control while
the real case remains blocked by missing reviewer or implementation. A complete
Q may honestly contain counter/conflict/pending. A fast invalid result is neither
correctness acceptance nor research improvement. Actual acceptance/disposition is
a later authorized S7 act, not generating this report.

at source inspection. Project HEAD is 8044732d505f84e373119a70f947df00e1767300;

repository HEAD remains cbf3f6861ef7cf66d8d02aaf71b1c89630eaf7fc. This does not
establish the full dependency/compiled/loaded closure or any runtime pass.

* SPECIFIED Causal-change correctness versus comparative research
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: s9-causal-correctness-and-research
:END:

qualified impact -> affected U/K/Q/R/decision/receiver-use eligibility -> real technical
recheck and any separately authorized resolution. Distinguish violated prerequisite,
missing observation, exact refutation, model/data/environment/measurement/population

stale use is blocked at its actual policy scope; unrelated complete formal/locator

Restored premise cannot resurrect revoked seal or erase denial. No identification
support is not zero effect, and trigger label is no authorized withdrawal.

The paper remains a research object. RQ1-RQ6 in 10.61 and receiver comparisons in
10.62 are hypotheses, not acceptance axioms or demonstrated improvements. Separate
research plan may compare manual/structured full revalidation, qualified dependency-
driven selective reuse and a general causal platform only if genuinely implemented
and justified. No invented weak paper reference implementation or automatic CRL label.

| Comparison requirement | Required protocol / honest report |
|-------------------------+-----------------------------------|
| Comparable basis | Same independently authored exogenous histories, ground truth, required claim/authority/policy scope, perturbations and resource envelope; each actual implementation variant has its own source/C/provenance. Do not fake identical C when code differs |
| Correctness prerequisite | Complete required finite gold and matched rejection/history controls per variant. False-current use, unjustified action or erased denial is a correctness defect; failures/unknowns are reported, never dropped as outliers |

| Decision criterion | Prefer simpler complete verification if selective closure misses affected uses, increases unjustified complexity/cost or lacks evidence of benefit. Claims of improvement require actual matched repeated evidence/uncertainty at bounded scope |

Research can report no advantage/refutation with valid mechanism controls; real case

close correctness, source/load or authority gates. No benchmark, trial or experiment
is run or fabricated here.
