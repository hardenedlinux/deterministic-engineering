* Problem: Closing the Total Network Eclipse Gap of Reactive Gossip
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: wqc169-problem
:STATUS: implemented-verified
:END:

Reactive witness gossip ([[file:10.166-verifiable-transparency-log-and-split-view-equivocation-defense.org][10.166 / B9]]) guarantees cryptographic accountability
*after* witnesses exchange Signed Tree Heads, yet an attacker who temporarily
eclipses a production release node from external witnesses could attempt to
deploy a backdoored branch before gossip reconnects.

Proactive *Threshold Witness Cosigning* (Castro & Liskov OSDI 1999 PBFT;
Syta et al. IEEE S&P 2016 CoSi; Yin et al. PODC 2019 HotStuff) closes this gap
before execution via the *Byzantine Quorum Intersection Theorem*: for =N=
registered witnesses tolerating =f= Byzantine double-signers, requiring a
threshold =t= with =2t - N >= f + 1= (e.g. =t = 3= of =N = 4= for =f = 1=)
guarantees that any two valid Quorum Certificates intersect in at least one
honest witness. Because honest witnesses verify RFC 6962 consistency and refuse
conflicting roots at the same tree size, conflicting finalized checkpoints
become mathematically impossible even under total network eclipse.

* Limits
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: wqc169-limits
:STATUS: implemented-verified
:END:

Quorum intersection finality holds provided strictly fewer than =f + 1=
registered witnesses are compromised simultaneously and the offline verifier
holds an authentic, out-of-band pinned witness roster. Production deployments
should distribute witness keys across independent administrative domains and
rotate rosters via threshold-signed governance transitions.
