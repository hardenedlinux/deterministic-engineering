* SPECIFIED Lifetime refinement for same-C long-running work
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: audit-capture-origin-lifetime
:END:

P3's capture-origin receipt is a bounded verification of an immutable actual
acquisition occurrence, not C itself and not a perpetual right to use it. Distinguish
original acquisition/issuance provenance, current verification interval and fresh
target-use eligibility. A 600s investigation envelope cannot assume its origin seal's
lease covers every final issuance/review; a caller cannot extend issued-at/expires-at.

The expected source-bound owner may freshly verify the same original basis/occurrence
only if its genuine acquisition/origin association is still retained and independently
checkable at that owner. Record the original origin plus new verification/use
observation in P1's existing acquisition/origin/current-use groups, declared before
closed validation; consume the new observation as a later input at the actual operation
boundary. Preserve C's captured meaning/identity and old receipt. Do not patch C,
revive the old private seal, add a second authoritative capture or admit arbitrary
C-shaped data. This is a target protocol refinement, not an existing new export.

Current source/status/role/premise/operation checks remain necessary. If original
association, required source stability or expected operation cannot be verified,
refuse that current use; report unavailable/expired origin rather than pretend a
renewal. A real new acquisition or imported qualified assertion has its own actual
origin and appropriate new C/target lineage. Process loss/foreign JSON cannot restore
the original owner. Recovered original bytes alone do not recreate private issuance.

If an actual workload already ran, retain A/E/raw outputs and exact launch/input/time
origin even when final current-use/issuance refuses. A historical diagnostic can remain
true without positive W/use. New target U or re-verification does not re-observe that
execution, rerun it by implication or append future verification to old C's self-hash.
Actual checkpoint/final guard and qualified clock/source semantics must be implemented;
if this route is absent, long-running positive use remains blocked, not auto-recaptured.

* SPECIFIED Correctness versus research hypotheses
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: audit-correctness-research
:END:

Mandatory correctness: exact source/issuer/capture/assertion binding, normalized
candidate retention, full independent demand, original negatives, sound scope-specific
invalidation, authentic actions/inclusion/current use, phase-typed closure and exact
receiver/effect permission. Every independently known stale use must block. Missing
capability/data/authority remains blocked/unsupported, never a default pass or zero.

Research hypotheses: better causal assessment/transport, smaller affected sets,
selective reuse, simpler approval organization, review quality/cost/latency or benefit
over paper-derived alternatives. Conservative full revalidation is valid unless an
actually selected Contract advertises a narrower bound. Paper is a research object,
not authority, a required platform or proof of our superiority. Compare real variants
on matched exogenous histories/requirements/gold/budgets with actual per-variant C and
all failures/unknowns, then paired cost/uncertainty. No fake identical C, invented weak
baseline, single contaminated run or unmeasured advantage. No advantage/refutation can
be an honest research outcome without failing correct conservative mechanics.

P9 separately reports mechanism/lane correctness, real case-positive readiness and
research results. Actual semantic reviewers/mandate/products may still block the case
when scoped mechanics hold. No empirical estimator is invented to close unrelated
architecture/ownership tests; empirical causal assertions need actual data/design at
required strength. These distinctions are now referenced explicitly by P1-P9.
