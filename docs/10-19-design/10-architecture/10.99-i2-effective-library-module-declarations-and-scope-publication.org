:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: i2-effective-version-inheritance

neutral metadata while fixing their own family meaning. Earlier family-local sole

role. Abstract Types publish their actual base roles and supported inert extension

associate them with the admitted family at Rust publication. The expected descriptor
comes from that trusted source/operation, not a submitted role name. Generic-only
selection must not import/demand Rust or organization authored objects merely to
enumerate a complete role registry; no such global registry is needed.

Read version through actual admitted Scope -> expected role descriptor ->
declaration-contract/family association -> inherited LibraryDeclarationContract
metadata. A role descriptor references that declaration owner; it has no copied
contract-version field. Definitions reference their expected role Contract. Objects,
Config, Module, Profile, Scenario, Org/D2 and generated reports do not author another
version literal. A submitted Contract slot/name/V1 string cannot choose its validator
or prove source ownership; expected descriptor and Core source/edge association agree.

Preserve actual semantic revisions ownership-budget/v1, backpressure/v3 and
backpressure-integration/v1 as opaque engineering meanings. They are not Library
V2/V3, Namespace suffixes or executable protocol versions. Actual new organization,
policy/catalog/mapping/method/Program revisions must be authored by their source

Git pins, source digests, Core generations, document sections and wire tags remain
separate identities. Descriptor mutation/stale source requires actual re-admission,
not a new Org-owned counter or a matching version-label exemption.
