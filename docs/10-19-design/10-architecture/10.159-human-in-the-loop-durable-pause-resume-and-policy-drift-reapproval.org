* Problem: Five Structural Failure Modes of Naive HITL Pause/Resume
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: hitl159-problem
:STATUS: implemented-verified
:END:

Long-horizon AI workflows frequently pause for hours or days awaiting human
approval (production rollouts, KMS rotations, schema changes, regulated sign-offs).
Naive coroutine-checkpointing or chat-interrupt frameworks fail along five axes:

1. *Stale-Approval TOCTOU Across Human Think-Time*: While a human sleeps, the
   repository baseline moves (=C0 -> C1=), a CVE advisory tightens security
   policy (=P0 -> P1=), or the approval TTL expires. Blindly resuming a cached
   "approved = true" flag executes an action invalid under the live world.
2. *Human Approval Fatigue & Social Engineering*: Feeding 40-page raw agent
   transcripts or persuasive prose ("URGENT, CEO approved over coffee!") to
   approvers induces rubber-stamping and prompt-injection bypasses.
3. *Hibernation Resource & Capability Leak (=R3=)*: Holding live =M3= leases/DB
   locks during multi-day pause starves peers, while serializing live =M3=
   handles into checkpoints violates Stratified Orthogonal Persistence
   ([[file:10.156-orthogonal-persistence-durable-multi-agent-governance.org][10.156]]).
4. *Duplicate-Webhook & Cross-Proposal Replay*: Double-clicking an approval
   button or replaying an approval receipt across proposals can duplicate
   non-idempotent external side effects.
5. *Agent Nagging / Human MFA-Bombing*: An autonomous retry loop that ignores a
   human "No" and keeps re-submitting slightly tweaked proposals exploits human
   fatigue until an accidental click occurs.

* Stratified OP Architecture for Scenario B5
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: hitl159-architecture
:STATUS: implemented-verified
:END:

| Governance Mechanism | Stratification & Enforcement | Guarantee |
|----------------------+------------------------------+-----------|
| Cold =M1=-Only Hibernation | =assertM1Persistable= (=R3=) + mandatory revocation of active =M3= lease before writing =SuspensionAnchor= | Zero live =M3= handles held or serialized during multi-day pause |
| Anti-Fatigue =HumanDecisionBrief= | Role-projected cold =M1= brief (diff digest, affected scopes, verified receipts, pinned rollback cut); strips 100% of untrusted =M2= agent prose | Eliminates social-engineering persuasion & approval-context overload |
| Cryptographically Bound =M1= Approval Receipt + =R1= Drift Co-Evolution | Receipt pins =(suspensionId, proposalHash, baseCutHash, policyEpochHash, expiresAtEpoch)=; on wake-up, baseline drift or intersecting policy drift fails closed (=PendingReapproval=), while provably disjoint policy updates succeed via =R1= | Zero stale-approval execution without unnecessary re-approval storms |
| Single-Use Consumption & Monotonic =M3= Execution Lease | Atomic consumption ledger + fresh single-use monotonic =M3= lease (=epoch' > suspendedEpoch=) scoped strictly to =proposalHash= | Duplicate clicks return =AlreadyConsumedNoop=; cross-proposal replay is =Refuted= |
| Multi-Party Quorum & Anti-Nagging Veto Persistence | Distinct-principal =k=-of-=N= SoD (=approverId != requesterId=) + immutable =M1= =HumanVetoConstraint= with finite re-submission budget requiring explicit =vetoResolutionEvidenceHash= | Prevents self-approval, duplicate-signer spoofing, and infinite nagging loops |

* Limits
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: hitl159-limits
:STATUS: implemented-verified
:END:

The in-process engine verifies cold hibernation, prose stripping, baseline/policy
drift invalidation, quorum SoD, idempotent consumption, and anti-nagging budgets.
Production deployment additionally requires cryptographic human identity signing
(WebAuthn/OIDC/HSM), durable outbox notification delivery to human channels, and
external clock/NTP monotonicity guarantees for TTL enforcement.
