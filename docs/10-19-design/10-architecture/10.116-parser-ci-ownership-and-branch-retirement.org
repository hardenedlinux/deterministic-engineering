* Correction: remove custom CI identity admission
The .gerbil/ci/identity design was a project-added duplicate admission layer,
not a Yuanxu/Core contract. It is removed, together with the five-field compare,
GERBIL_PREPARED_HEAD and prepared-products.sh pack/restore modes. The earlier
identity-file refusal control tests retired machinery and is withdrawn as
framework acceptance evidence. This correction supersedes earlier statements
that transport identity comparison is a required admission layer.

GitHub Actions binds the artifact to its workflow run/platform/name; the existing
composite activates the compiler, downloads that artifact and extracts it with
native tar preserving modes/timestamps. Core TestingScript independently checks
its captured inputs, outputs and approvals when consuming test support. Ordinary
Gerbil compilation and existing runtime/source qualification keep their distinct
claims. No replacement identity manifest, loader or credential is introduced.
The remaining failed-products.sh has only diagnostic-custody responsibility and
is not used by successful transport or framework admission.
