* DESIGN Objective and occurrence boundary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: s8-library-delivery-recipient-use
:STATUS: Existing-file/field/call/acceptance design only; no implementation, execution or actual delivery
:END:

S8 carries an exact identified Library publication and inspectable reviewed basis
to an authenticated recipient/project for a declared purpose. Separate finalized
manifest, distribution authorization, actual arrival, receiver verification,
Library adoption and current intended use. Package import, transport success,
producer review or a signature establishes none of the later acts by itself.

Producer C0/Q0/R0 and A/E/G/W/use/history remain immutable. Receiver forms its own
C1/Q1/R1 using actual eligible publication, project inputs, policy/roles and current
trust. The indices distinguish occurrences/owners, not versions or new Contract
generations. Receiver can reuse qualified knowledge/evidence at matching strength;
it cannot reuse producer project facts or deserialize producer private authority.
No new transport service, grant directory, receipt issuer, fact store or global
adoption/deduplication database is introduced.

The 75 selected implementation/build/runner files still match S0-S7 at inspection.
Project HEAD remains 8044732d505f84e373119a70f947df00e1767300; linked dirty Core is
52130d2d338b09622a3f55e37987ddc0375b6187; Aitia own repository HEAD remains
cbf3f6861ef7cf66d8d02aaf71b1c89630eaf7fc. Actual source/load/currentness matters
beyond HEAD. No real receiver repository, adoption mandate, channel authority or
complete cross-host protocol is inferred. WP00/BS01-BS09 remain open.

* SPECIFIED Product/reference closure and provisioning boundary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: s8-product-closure-and-provisioning
:END:

Independent delivery demand comes from actual adopted policy/publication/purpose;
receiver also supplies its own expected demand. D01-D10 roles may share sections.
Include exact model/mapping/architecture/Org/decision products, Q explanation and
source/claim provenance, required results/negatives/denials, actual review/authority,
source/build/dependency instructions and current status/access references. Offered
file list cannot define completeness. Instructions are inert documentation.

Reuse S4b finite typed demand closure over actual retained inputs. Bind every
required edge/unique selector and full-file byte identity, owning base, relocation,
source-copy origin, symlink/alias/path policy, access/retention and finite budgets.
Cyclic references require bounded traversal retaining incoming demands; ambiguous,
unsupported, unretained or inaccessible mandatory target leaves a gap. A producer-
local .data/worktree link or checksum of an offered subset is not accessible
closure. Confidentiality may require a real controlled-access route, not verified
content by absence. Genuine bounded S7 exception retains unmet duty visibly.

Intake never fetches arbitrary links, evaluates Org/Scheme initializers, runs build
scripts or follows archive paths into executable actions. Explicitly authorized
provisioning occurs later at actual host/Core Ops/source owners. Source-only
inspection remains possible while compiled adoption is blocked. Compiled delivery
needs qualified source-to-build-to-artifact/runtime/platform/dependency provenance
or actual qualified receiver rebuild, not matching filename/source hash/version.
Finite ABI test is one check, not universal semantic compatibility. New checked
receiver publication must retain S2 normalized parent/Scope/function associations.

issuer/custody/status and verifier; local verification has a new actual origin.
It does not re-observe old workload execution. Without such route, retain historical
provenance/gaps and perform required actual receiver observations/executions.

stay counters; M06 completion or Lean negation is no positive budget/ownership

* SPECIFIED Causal applicability across recipients and later changes
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: s8-causal-applicability-research
:END:

Continue S7's RQ1-RQ6: the CSE paper is a research object, not a prescribed delivery
architecture or evidence of our superiority. In particular, transferring an
artifact, an accepted producer assertion and a causal claim's validity to a new
environment are different questions. The following is our candidate design.

| Claim / change | Required receiver comparison and result boundary |
|----------------+---------------------------------------------------|
| Descriptive or formal/model claim | Bind exact subject/domain/premises. An arithmetic theorem may remain reusable at identical assumptions without empirical re-estimation; a finite path result stays finite/model-scoped |
| Empirical effect or incident counterfactual | Compare actual intervention/comparator/outcome definitions/units, measurement, population/workload/topology/time window, identification/assignment/confounding/interference/stability conditions and data/model/estimator basis. Same Library generation/model name cannot establish transfer |
| Qualified compatible environment within supported domain | Actual supported transport/compatibility argument and method/verifier yields fresh target use at declared strength, or new bounded analysis. No automatic assertion of transportability from a metadata match |
| Known failed premise or changed identification conditions | Block affected empirical support/use, retain original W/producer acceptance historically, trigger actual scoped re-evaluation. Failed identification is not zero effect or fabricated contrary W |
| Missing receiver measurement/status/assumption evidence | Keep unsupported/unestablished use with exact required unit. Absence is not proven premise violation, false effect or permission to proceed |
| New causal refutation or uncertainty/decision-condition failure | Retain qualified comparison/contrary findings and affect exact use/adoption/decision conditions under real policy; no favorable-only report or automatic signed revocation |
| Premise repair/restoration or authorized bounded exception | Actual new evidence/current technical review and S7 resolution as required; no revived old seal, erased denial or exception-as-truth. Non-exceptable identity/authority conditions still apply |

Map actual assumption/claim/source-cut -> original W -> receiver U/K/Q1/R1 ->
adoption/current operation conditions, retaining both origins. Qualified dependency
cone may restrict recheck; absent complete real closure, use conservative impact.
Independent unaffected formal/locator findings need not become false because a
separate empirical estimate loses applicability. No actor-defined trigger label
or an unavailable external status service grants decision authority.

Research comparison: on matched producer/receiver histories with independent
known affected claims, compare complete receiver revalidation, qualified selective
reuse and manual review under identical policy requirements. Check false-current
uses and unjustified actions before measuring false-blocked uses, rework/latency
and trust/custody complexity. Reject selectivity if dependencies are missed or cost
exceeds justified benefit; retain simpler full revalidation where sufficient.
Do not claim improvement against an invented weak paper implementation. No such
comparison, population estimate or actual recipient experiment is executed here.

A notification is advisory until actual status is verified by receiver; a recipient
list is not notice delivery. S8 defines future authorized association only and
sends nothing here. Causal applicability loss and distribution withdrawal need
not have identical scope or target owner. An actual authorized publication/use
withdrawal is a separate act from mechanical discovery of an invalid premise.

Check-at-use and effect are not automatically atomic. Actual effect owner binds
principal/action/immutable inputs/source/status through qualified authorization/
serialization/fencing or refuses operations requiring stronger guarantees. No
before/after hash proves universal CAS or closed races. Later revocation cannot
undo an executed operation; rollback/containment is another authorized effect
retaining original outcome. No global exactly-once or instantaneous disconnected
recipient invalidation is promised.
