* DESIGN Objective and research boundary
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: s7-history-authority-reassessment
:STATUS: File/field/call/research/acceptance design only; no implementation, authority operation or review submission
:END:

S7 binds actual C/Q/R and current S6 technical use to authoritative history H,
authenticates action-specific roles/grants, accepts records only through actual
custody owners, and reassesses or withdraws affected current use without deleting
counter evidence or denials. Repair, exception, denial supersession and revocation
remain distinct. Authored DE-D01-D05 are design choices, not accepted authority
records. No policy definition, paper citation, proof or receipt appoints a principal.

Causal Software Engineering is our research object, not a normative architecture
source. Our proposed extension is an explicit lifecycle for supported claim
assumptions and their effect on evidence, review and authority under change. Its
benefits over simpler alternatives are hypotheses requiring comparative evidence.
This document does not claim superior causal inference or empirical results.

Library owns permitted claim/coverage/history/role/action meaning; Scenario owns
actual project bindings; producers establish technical claims; Aitia owns common
discovery/action/current-use semantics; source/custody owners accept and preserve
records; Core owns graph algorithms and local receipt primitives. ASCENT projects
accepted associations only; Runtime/recipient owners decide their own effects/use.
No new ledger, grant directory, consensus service or universal revocation engine.

The 75 selected implementation/build/runner files still match S0-S6 at inspection.
Project HEAD remains 8044732d505f84e373119a70f947df00e1767300; linked dirty Core is

* DESIGN Research questions, competing choices and falsification
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: s7-research-questions
:END:

[[https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02454v1][Causal Software Engineering: A Vision and Roadmap]], Sections 2-4, discusses
explicit assumptions, evolving models, governance and evaluation under change.
It leaves an open engineering question for this project: how should an observed
assumption change invalidate a particular use while preserving historical evidence
and legitimately accepted negative decisions? The following is our analysis of
that design problem, not a claim that the paper implemented a deficient protocol.

| Research question / candidate refinement | Alternative and proposed comparison / rejection condition |
|------------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------|
| RQ1: Is causal framing appropriate for every engineering claim? | Compare uniform causal treatment with typed descriptive/formal/model/empirical/normative claims. Reject our split if it permits empirical overclaim or adds ambiguity without reducing irrelevant obligations; no arrow/proof becomes empirical cause |
| RQ2: Can explicit assumption-to-use dependencies improve change handling? | Compare conservative complete revalidation with selective qualified dependency propagation on identical event histories. Both must block every independently known stale use; measure false-current and false-blocked uses, invalidation scope, rework and latency. Reject selectivity if closure misses a dependency or costs exceed the justified benefit |
| RQ3: Does separating evidence truth, review eligibility and action authority prevent invalid reuse? | Compare explicit boundaries with a simpler equivalent scoped approval design on expiry, policy/role change, late denial and repaired evidence. Equal inputs/requirements are essential; keep the simpler design if equally correct with less complexity |
| RQ4: What can counterfactual benchmark labels establish? | Separate known intervention/model ground truth, independently observed effects and incident narrative labels. A narrative can test explanation agreement, not alone validate counterfactual truth. Reject causal-strength claims without appropriate identification or experimental/model basis |
| RQ5: What happens when identification or transport conditions fail? | Compare honest unsupported/current-use blocking with a declared bounded fallback policy. Measure unjustified action, retained uncertainty and cost; neither automatic zero-effect conclusion nor a receipt can repair identification |
| RQ6: Can negative history remain usable through repair and revocation? | Use independent event-sequence gold for denial -> technical repair -> authorized resolution -> resolution withdrawal. Check immutable origins and exact reopening, not only final positive counts; reject designs that erase denial or revive stale seals |

These are proposed experimental questions, not executed acceptance or performance
results. A straightforward artifact-based realization of the paper is a possible
baseline, not a known reference implementation. Do not invent a weak paper baseline
to claim superiority. Candidate choices are (a) manual structured review with full
revalidation, (b) qualified dependency-driven claim/use lifecycle, or (c) a general
causal-model/estimation platform. We provisionally choose (b) for this Library's
existing owner boundaries, with conservative full revalidation whenever dependency
closure is unqualified. Choice (c) requires evidence of actual domain need and
qualified estimators; it is not justified by the paper alone. Choice (a) remains
valid if it meets correctness/cost requirements more simply.

A future research protocol must fix input histories, independent ground truth,
comparison requirements and resource budgets, retain all failures/unknowns, and
report correctness before selective cost. Paper terminology or CRL labels are not
our acceptance metric. Our six-case ownership mechanism tests bounded technical
and authority transitions, not population-level latency effect or real incident
prevention; such claims need separately supported data/design/methods. No experiment
or benchmark is run here. The main S7 design below is a candidate to evaluate.

* SPECIFIED Required history discovery and negative preservation
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: s7-history-discovery
:END:

Actual adopted project mandate/source custody defines relevant technical/counter/
failure, decision/denial/exception/withdrawal, role/grant/key-status and predecessor
sources independently of submitter. Genuine initial state needs authenticated
no-predecessor/source mandate, not an absent directory treated as clean history.

Discovery resolves actual selected heads/prefixes/accepted inventories and original
origin/signature/grant/lineage through qualified source operations, with owned
values and declared finite budgets. Conflicting identities/forked heads, missing
predecessor, unavailable source or older/smaller prefix blocks required completeness.
Retain offending candidates and all known accepted records. H is an observed source
vector, not proof of global absence or atomic distributed time. Unsupported stronger
freshness/consistency is a gap; offline grace requires actual bounded adopted policy.

| Record/source state | Required retained meaning |
|----------------------+--------------------------|
| Accepted authenticated counter/denial | Preserve original claim/scope/revision/policy/origin across favorable evidence or policy changes |
| Accepted inventory names unavailable negative proof | Incomplete discovery/current authority; absence cannot become approval or delete the known record |
| Untrusted malformed/forged rejection | Diagnostic intake only, not accepted denial merely because its text says rejected |
| Source removed/shrunk/forked or new policy excludes old scope | Require actual continuity/retirement/lineage/impact authority and explicit relevance reasoning; no reset by new label/latest timestamp/hash |
| New accepted record/status after H | Old H remains historical; actual current use rediscovers/reverifies relevant source cuts |

Each operation verifies its declared discovery prerequisites. Positive admission
requires complete relevant history and technical conditions; legitimate negative/
defer/withdraw actions have their own authenticated target/source/authority checks
and must not require the positive technical success they deny. This does not let
unauthorized or unbound rejection text become accepted history.

| Accept / actual custody owner | Verified exact record and expected prior selected head -> accepted immutable prefix/head or refusal/unknown; recheck source/grant/current basis under qualified serialization |
| Assess use / Aitia | Current H plus original accepted decision and fresh technical/role/status/receipt use -> scoped usable/blocked/unknown; old preflight cannot establish accepted inclusion |
| Reassess / project + method/content/Aitia | Old basis/negatives and actual change/repair/assumption findings -> new scoped C/use/Q/R/H as affected and authorized resolution or remaining blockers |
| Withdraw/revoke / target owner | Authenticated exact target action -> retained status/current-use effect; local receipt revoke and accepted project withdrawal are different operations |

A grant/delegation chain cannot widen parent actions/scope/validity or allow
unauthorized redelegation. Reject cycles, ambiguous roots, incorrect rotation,
unsupported conditions and false quorum. Distinct keys/signatures from one principal
are not distinct principals. Historical validity at record acceptance and current
permission are separate: ordinary expiry need not erase old denial, while targeted
withdrawal/compromise must affect use according to actual policy. Caller backdating,
self-selected trust root or public technical.admitted? cannot establish any of this.

Positive admission needs actual policy-defined technical/context/content/roles and
permitted conditions/exceptions. Negative/defer/withdraw actions retain truthful
incomplete basis, do not require positive technical success, and grant no positive
permission. Reuse genuine Host preflight/Cedar mapping only at its qualified action;
preflight/technical support remains distinct from accepted decision/Runtime effect.

Decision binds immutable C/Q/R and predecision H0; actual append yields H1; current
H2 verifies original H0 lineage, accepted inclusion and later relevant records.
Never sign a future prefix containing the signature itself or insert the new
decision into its own Q/R input hash. Postacceptance assertion is separate from
proposal verification even for equal record bytes.

Expected prior -> verified action -> write/close/sign/head selection -> current
accepted prefix is serialized by actual host custody at its qualified scope. Hash
comparison or rename alone is not cross-host CAS/durability. If head/grant/basis
changes before acceptance, refuse/reprepare. If an accepted negative is known but
publication fails/ambiguous, retain it and block positive use of that owner until
genuine recovery; never rewind to favorable old frame. Other genuine operations
and receipts are not rolled back across owners. Unknown completion stays unknown.

* SPECIFIED Repair, exception, supersession and withdrawal
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: s7-resolution-and-withdrawal
:END:

| Operation | Exact obligation / retained limit |
|-----------+-----------------------------------|
| Technical repair | New supported A/E/W/use and old-new claim/domain/correspondence plus independent rationale; retain old counter/failure. A fix for M04 cannot repair M06 or unrelated content adequacy |
| Missing evidence supplied | Actual new qualified observation/coverage creates affected Q/R; old missing/timeout history is not rewritten |
| Exception | Actual policy permits exact bounded unmet duty and risk authority/mitigation/expiry/reopen. False remains false; cannot waive identity/authentic authority/truth or non-exceptable conditions |
| Denial supersession / decision replacement | Exact accepted prior IDs, scope/lineage/impact, genuine authority to replace each affected denial and explicit new accepted record. Favorable result, new policy label or unsigned forward ref cannot replace it |
| Resolution withdrawn/ineligible | Recompute affected live coverage; original denial blocks again unless another actual currently eligible scoped resolution covers it |
| Receipt/use/grant/decision/owner withdrawal | Dispatch to actual issuer/producer/project trust/custody owner, retain distinct target/status/effective scope and current consequences; no generic erase toggle |

Use Core's pure cycle/dependency algorithms on qualified scope-correct replacement
edges; algorithm success authenticates none. Supersession must be acyclic and
unambiguous; independent concurrent approval/rejection remain retained until real
policy-authorized resolution. Technical repair and accepted denial replacement
are separate acts. Accepted exceptions are not property support or deployment
permission and expire when required conditions fail.

Fresh current-time Core admission and S6 source/clock/status checks apply at each
actual use. Old admission is fixed at its old instant; valid frozen seal does not
rediscover live grants/decisions. Returning old bytes cannot revive known revoked
receipts or retired owners. Status withdrawal propagation to recipients is not
instantaneous global revocation; S8/recipient verifies its own current conditions.

Recovery requires genuine selected closed prefix/origins/trust/lineage, fresh actual
reader/activation and known predecessor retirement. Exact authorized storage-prefix
repair preserves all accepted content/signatures; it changes no project claim or
authority decision. Replayed old repair/head/activation after current selection
advances refuses. Local fresh reader bookkeeping is not global single-reader proof.
No new recovery run or external notification occurs here.

* SPECIFIED Causal assumption invalidation and reassessment transitions
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: s7-assumption-impact-transitions
:END:

Assumption declarations belong to selected criterion/model/project basis; observed
status comes from actual qualified checks. Status is contextual to a claim/domain/
source cut, not a global true/false flag on an assumption name. Retain exact dependency
relations through original W/target U/required K/Q/R and accepted decision conditions.
Conservative impact is required where those relations are not qualified; do not
claim minimal propagation merely because Core can compute a dependency cone.

| Trigger / observed state | Required technical/review/authority response |
|--------------------------+---------------------------------------------|
| Known prerequisite violated (e.g. declared isolated intervention changed concurrently) | Block affected causal use at its real currentness/policy owner; retain original W/history. Lack of identification is not proof of no effect or automatic opposite W |
| Evidence for prerequisite unavailable, stale or check incomplete | Mark affected support/use unestablished at declared scope; preserve reason and required unit. A missing measurement is not a verified assumption violation or positive claim |
| New qualified result contradicts exact claim | Retain scoped counter alongside support; S5 compares actual claim/basis before conflict, S6/S7 retain unresolved findings and block unsupported positive use |
| Model, data, measurement, population, environment, interference or transport scope changes | Qualify impact and supported transfer/identification; new C/Q/R or fresh use as actually required. Same title/hash/revision label cannot transfer an empirical effect |
| Sensitivity/robustness deteriorates or uncertainty violates actual decision condition | Record actual finding/limits and re-evaluate affected criterion/decision-use. Do not invent an effect interval or infer universal invalidity from one heuristic |
| New qualified repair/design/experiment resolves prerequisite | Actual new method/content finding and current applicable basis, then S5/S6 review and S7 resolution if needed; old denial/counter remains and does not disappear automatically |
| Assumption recovers, or resolution is withdrawn | Actual fresh supported verification can restore eligible technical use; accepted denial still needs genuine current resolution. Revoked historical receipt is never resurrected |
| Noncausal grant/role/history/custody failure | Apply exact authority/source invalidation regardless of whether causal estimate is unchanged; technical validity cannot override missing permission |

Operational sequence: observe actual qualified change -> identify affected original
claim and dependent uses/decision conditions -> invalidate/block affected current
eligibility under actual policy -> retain diagnosis/unknown/counter separately ->
acquire/recheck at actual method/content owners -> new qualified C/use/Q/R as affected
-> rediscover current H -> authenticate any required action/resolution -> actual
acceptance -> fresh current-use check. Trigger data is not a signed decision or
agent mandate; automatic technical eligibility change and authorized decision
withdrawal are separate, each at its real owner.

Whole-chain lifetime: changed captured meaning/project/selection/mandate inputs
requires new C; changed technical demands/evidence/query interpretation requires
new Q/R; history-only status/inclusion with unchanged technical meaning may use
same C/Q/R with new H/current use. Any query claiming the new history must itself
have a new accepted cut/Q. No mutation of prior C/Q/R/H/W or rerun of unrelated
Rust/QNT/Lean/SysML merely to rediscover a record.

For a network retry-policy example, detected concurrent scaling may defeat a
particular effect identification premise without showing retries have zero effect.
Reassess that empirical claim and any approval conditioned on it; do not revoke
unrelated formal ownership evidence or invent an incident counterfactual. For
DE-CASE-01, E06/I04 -> T03 -> DE-D04 remains the first architecture/capture chain.
Its assumptions can trigger scoped source/capture review; that does not establish
latency effects. Unassigned actual roles, optional T07, historical 20-result/22-record
subjects, fixed recovery failures and missing empirical data remain explicit.
