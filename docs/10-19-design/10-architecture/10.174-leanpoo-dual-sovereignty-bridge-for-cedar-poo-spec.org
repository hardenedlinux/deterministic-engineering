* Architectural Boundary: Library vs. Consuming Authorization Host
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: arch10174-boundary
:END:

In our multi-repository formal ecosystem, responsibilities are cleanly factored:

- **=deterministic-engineering-qnt= (Reusable Library — Lean 4 + =LeanPoo=, zero Mathlib, zero Cedar dependency)**:
  Owns domain-agnostic formal mechanisms, unbounded algebraic/inductive proofs,
  cryptographic quorum finality (=B10=), dynamic handover (=B10b=), slashing
  tombstones (=B10c=), stratified =M1/M2/M3= orthogonal persistence (=R1=–=R5=),
  and reusable =LeanPoo= contract refinement & proof-transport combinators.
- **=cedar-poo-spec= (Consuming Authorization Host — Lean 4 + =Cedar= + =LeanPoo=)**:
  Owns concrete Cedar authorization policies (=Forbid=-overrides-=Permit=),
  principal/role/tenant rules, and =CedarPooSpec.Admission.*= receipts
  (=BoundOperation=, =OperationIdentity=, =PolicySnapshot=, =AuthorityConsumption=).

Phase 26 formalizes the **Dual-Sovereignty Conjunction Bridge** connecting
both repositories with zero policy leakage into the Library.
