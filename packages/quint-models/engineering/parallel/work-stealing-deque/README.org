* Emergent Safety Invariant

Every pushed task is taken at most once across concurrent owner pop and thief steal operations.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = ((h.ownerPopped + h.thiefStolen) <= h.pushed and h.top <= h.bottom)
#+end_src
