* Emergent Safety Invariant

On-disk data pages never overtake the persisted WAL (write-ahead rule), and crash recovery preserves every commit acknowledged to clients.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.dataDiskVer <= h.walDiskVer and (not(h.crashed) or h.recoveredVer >= h.ackedCommitVer))
#+end_src
