* Emergent Safety Invariant

Leader Completeness: once a slot-1 entry is committed, every subsequent elected leader contains that exact committed value.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (not(h.committed) or h.leaderId == 0 or leaderLogVal == h.committedVal)
#+end_src
