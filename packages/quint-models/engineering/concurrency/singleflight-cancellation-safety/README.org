* Emergent Safety Invariant

Whenever a single-flight leader is cancelled or dropped mid-await, its RAII drop guard resets inFlight and wakes all waiters; every waiter arms notification prior to checking inFlight so zero lost-wakeup deadlocks can occur.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = ((not(h.inFlight) or h.leaderActive) and h.stuckWaiters == 0 and (h.inFlight or h.armedWaiters == 0))
#+end_src
