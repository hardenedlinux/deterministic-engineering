* Emergent Safety Invariant

Long-lived projection/session/artifact tables stay within their entry cap, reclaim idle slots on capacity pressure without permanent lockout, and cascade-delete auxiliary side maps with zero orphaned keys.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.entries <= 2 and h.activeCount <= h.entries and h.orphanedSideMap == 0)
#+end_src
