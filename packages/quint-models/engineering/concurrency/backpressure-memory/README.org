* Safety Invariant

Every admitted job belongs to exactly one mutually disjoint ownership state
(=queued=, =running=, =returned=, or =cleanup=), total admitted jobs never
exceed =limit=, queued jobs never exceed =queueLimit=, and completed (=done=)
jobs hold no active permit.

* Explicit Limits

Project-owned bounded abstraction over 5 discrete job IDs; makes no Rust
scheduler, allocator, or kernel memory-reclamation claim.
