* Emergent Safety Invariant

Congestion-aware load shedding and an explicit retry budget keep admitted queue depth and pending retry amplification within safe recovery bounds.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.queueDepth <= 2 and h.retriesPending <= 1 and h.retriesPending <= h.queueDepth)
#+end_src
