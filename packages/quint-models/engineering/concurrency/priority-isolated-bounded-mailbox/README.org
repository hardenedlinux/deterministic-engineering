* Emergent Safety Invariant

Separate per-tier bounded channels shed saturated low-priority bursts without starving high-priority queries, cancelled callers are actively compacted from queue slots, weighted fair draining avoids starvation, and latest-wins progress snapshots stay within O(1) slot.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.hiQueue <= 2 and h.loQueue <= 2 and not(h.hiStarved) and h.zombieLo == 0 and h.progressSlots <= 1 and h.observedWater <= h.producedWater)
#+end_src
