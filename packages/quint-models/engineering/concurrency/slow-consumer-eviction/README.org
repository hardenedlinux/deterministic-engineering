* Emergent Safety Invariant

Per-consumer fan-out queue never exceeds its cap and evicting a stalled consumer drains all retained frames with zero orphaned buffers.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.slowBuf <= 2 and h.orphanedBuf == 0 and (h.slowConnected or h.slowBuf == 0))
#+end_src
