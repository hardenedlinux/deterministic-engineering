* Emergent Safety Invariant

Parent scope exits only after every spawned child task completes draining, preventing orphaned effects after scope close.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (not(h.orphanEffect) and (not(h.parentExited) or childrenQuiescent))
#+end_src
