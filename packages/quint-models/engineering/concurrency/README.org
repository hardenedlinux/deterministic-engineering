* Overview

This subdomain groups co-located formal models for structured concurrency,
distributed lease fencing, compensating saga workflows, and bounded backpressure
ownership. Scheme-authored models expose Yuanxu Design 165 interfaces alongside
their projected =.qnt= and =README.org= files.

* Yuanxu Selection & Reuse
