* Emergent Safety Invariant

Step A executes at most once despite network retries, and once compensation aborts the saga, net applied effects stay strictly zero even under late in-flight deliveries.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.debitCount <= 1 and h.refundCount <= h.debitCount and (not(h.sagaAborted) or h.debitCount == h.refundCount))
#+end_src
