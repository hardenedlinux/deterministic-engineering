* Emergent Safety Invariant

Missing expected body (SetupDefect), incomplete method/scope (PendingMethod/PendingScope), and unresolved contradictory records (Conflict) never collapse into Proven (3) or Refuted (4).

#+begin_src quint
val safety = ((h.bodyPresent or (h.status != 3 and h.status != 4)) and ((h.methodDone and h.scopeKnown) or (h.status != 3 and h.status != 4)) and (not((h.hasAllow and h.hasDeny)) or h.conflictResolved or (h.status != 3 and h.status != 4)))
#+end_src
