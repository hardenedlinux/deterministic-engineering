* Emergent Safety Invariant

Forensic evidence stays intact across rollbacks, no author self-approves production promotion (sodViolations == 0), no promotion occurs without a pinned M1 pre-release cut (unpinnedPromotions == 0), and no promotion bypasses independent audit, emergency freeze, or canary SLO health (revokedOrBreachedPromotions == 0).
