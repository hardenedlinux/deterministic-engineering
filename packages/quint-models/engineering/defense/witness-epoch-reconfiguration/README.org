* Emergent Safety Invariant

Epoch 2 handover requires BOTH Q_old and Q_new ratification (unilateralHandoverViolations == 0), Epoch 2 checkpoint finality requires RFC 6962 anchor consistency from Epoch 1 (unanchoredEpoch2Violations == 0), and once Epoch 2 is pinned, posterior compromise of retired Epoch 1 keys cannot introduce a post-handover Epoch 1 fork (not(epoch2CleanFinalized and epoch1PostHandoverForkAccepted) and longRangeRewriteViolations == 0).

#+end_src
