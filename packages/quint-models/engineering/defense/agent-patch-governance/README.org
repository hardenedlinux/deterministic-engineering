* Emergent Safety Invariant

Repair attempts stay within bound (repairAttempts <= 2), and no AI Agent patch reaches Admitted (decision == 1) without independent mechanical audit, intact Band-5 floors (not floorWeakened), and preserved or explicitly replaced verification obligations.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.repairAttempts <= 2 and (h.decision != 1 or (h.independentAuditPassed and not(h.floorWeakened) and (not(h.obligationDeleted) or h.replacementAdmitted))))
#+end_src
