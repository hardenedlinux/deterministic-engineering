* Emergent Safety Invariant

During concurrent Mark phase, every mutator has enabled its write barrier and no unbarriered store occurs.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (not(h.unbarrieredWrite) and (h.phase != 2 or (h.m1Barrier and h.m2Barrier)))
#+end_src
