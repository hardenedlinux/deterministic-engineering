* Emergent Safety Invariant

Strong tri-color invariant holds during marking (no Black->White edge) and sweep never reclaims a reachable live object.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (not((h.sweptC and liveC)) and (h.phase != 0 or not((h.colorA == 2 and h.aToC and h.colorC == 0))))
#+end_src
