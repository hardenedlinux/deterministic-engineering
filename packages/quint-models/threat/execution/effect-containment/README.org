* Emergent Safety Invariant

No signature occurs while delegated, paused, revoked, or clear-pending; clearance requires verified finalized witness.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.signatures <= MaxSignatures and (h.signatures == 0 or (not(h.lastSignedPaused) and not(h.lastSignedDelegated))) and (not(h.cleared) or h.witness))
#+end_src
