* Emergent Safety Invariant

Prepared effects commit durably under current authority and emit at most MaxSends times while retaining unknown outcomes.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.sent <= MaxSends and (h.sent == 0 or (h.durable and h.unknown)))
#+end_src
