* Emergent Safety Invariant

External room ingress never forges origin or gate receipts and never injects across channels; approval gates resolve strictly once by a human operator; gate-blocked matters cannot be unblocked by AI agents; and untrusted external messages never trigger privileged tools without human gate approval.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (not(h.forgedGateReceipt) and not(h.crossChannelInjected) and not(h.gateReplayed) and not(h.agentSelfUnblocked) and not(h.taintedToolBypass))
#+end_src
