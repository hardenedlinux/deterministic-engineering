* Emergent Safety Invariant

Active egress flows require attested non-quarantined sidecar mediation and explicit cross-tenant authorization when crossing tenant boundaries.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.activeFlowDest == 0 or (h.sidecarAttested and not(h.quarantined) and (h.activeFlowDest == h.tenantId or h.crossTenantAllowed)))
#+end_src
