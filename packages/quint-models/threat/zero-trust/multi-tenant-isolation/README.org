* Emergent Safety Invariant

Tenant-scoped tickets never enforce against a foreign tenant resource or after tenant membership revocation, and queued offline outbox items never flush into a different active tenant.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (not(h.crossTenantLeak) and not(h.staleTenantUse) and not(h.crossTenantFlush))
#+end_src

* Explicit Limits
