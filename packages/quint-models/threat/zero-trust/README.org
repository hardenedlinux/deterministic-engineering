* Overview

This subdomain groups co-located Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA) and policy
governance threat models: authority currentness predicates, PEP/PDP policy-epoch
split, cross-tenant microsegmentation egress mediation, Separation of Duties
with veto precedence, and cumulative k-anonymity disclosure budgets.
