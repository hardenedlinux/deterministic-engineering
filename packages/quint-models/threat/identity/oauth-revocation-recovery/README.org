* Emergent Safety Invariant

Token admission requires PAR binding, and once revocation intent is requested, crash-recovery never restores an active session.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (not(h.sessionActive) or (h.parRegistered and not(h.revokeRequested)))
#+end_src

- *T. Lodderstedt, B. Campbell, N. Sakimura, D. Tonge & F. Skokan (IETF RFC 9126, 2021)*: OAuth 2.0 Pushed Authorization Requests (PAR): authenticated pre-registration of authorization parameters.
- *T. Lodderstedt, S. Dronia & M. Scurtescu (IETF RFC 7009, 2013)*: OAuth 2.0 Token Revocation: durable sign-out intent across host crashes.
