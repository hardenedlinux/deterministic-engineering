* Emergent Safety Invariant

Authorization codes are single-use with PKCE+nonce, refresh never resurrects a signed-out/revoked vault entry, and refreshed scopes never escalate beyond granted scopes.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = ((h.signedIn or not(h.vaultHasToken)) and h.activeScopeLevel <= h.grantedScopeLevel and h.exchanges <= 1)
#+end_src
