* Emergent Safety Invariant

Every accepted WebAuthn login binds to the exact RP origin, consumes a unique ceremony challenge once, and advances the authenticator signCount monotonically.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.totalLogins == 0 or (h.lastAcceptedOrigin == h.rpOrigin and h.totalLogins == size(h.consumedChallenges) and h.lastAcceptedCounter > h.prevStoredCounter))
#+end_src
