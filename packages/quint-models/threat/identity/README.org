* Overview

This subdomain groups co-located formal threat models for human and workload
identity protocols: OAuth 2.1 lifecycle & PKCE, PAR & crash-safe revocation
recovery, OpenID Connect federation, RFC 8628 device authorization, WebAuthn /
FIDO2 passkey ceremonies, and short-lived SPIFFE/SVID rotation.
