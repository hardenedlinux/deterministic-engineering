* Emergent Safety Invariant

Delegated agent tokens never amplify human scope, never impersonate direct human authority on human-only gates, fail closed immediately on human session revocation, and privileged operations require verified identity plus fresh step-up authentication.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (not(h.scopeAmplified) and not(h.impersonatedHuman) and not(h.zombieDelegatedUse) and not(h.unverifiedPrivileged))
#+end_src
