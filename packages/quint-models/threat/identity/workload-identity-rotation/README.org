* Emergent Safety Invariant

Emergency revocation overrides any overlap/TTL window; no active workload session runs on a revoked or expired credential generation.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (not(h.sessionActive) or (h.sessionGen != h.revokedGen and not(h.sessionExpired)))
#+end_src
