* Emergent Safety Invariant

Client never installs a target older than its monotonic snapshot floor, never accepts expired timestamp metadata, and always binds artifact hash atomically to its snapshot.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (h.installedVer >= h.clientMinVer and h.installedMetaExp >= h.installedAtClock and h.installedHash == h.installedSnapTargetVer)
#+end_src
