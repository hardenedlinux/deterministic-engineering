* Emergent Safety Invariant

Every artifact accepted by a client appears in the publicly audited append-only log, preventing both unlogged binaries and split-view equivocation.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = h.acceptedArtifacts.subseteq(h.publicLog)
#+end_src
