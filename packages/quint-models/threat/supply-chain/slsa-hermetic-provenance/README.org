* Emergent Safety Invariant

Every deployed artifact carries builder-signed provenance and matches the exact reviewed source digest, even if the primary builder is compromised and signs Trojan provenance.

#+begin_src quint
val safety = (not(h.deployed) or (h.deployedArtifactDigest == h.reviewedSrcDigest and h.deployedProvenanceSigned))
#+end_src
